Who we are

A ministry of the Coptic Orthodox Church, open to everyone

Upper Room is a not-for-profit ministry built to encourage spiritual growth, Christian learning and genuine fellowship, whatever your background.

It's a place where faith and daily life meet: a carefully chosen selection of Christian books and gifts that help deepen your understanding of Scripture, tradition and the life of the Church.

More than a bookshop, Upper Room is a place to pause. To read quietly, talk over coffee, or simply sit in a moment of stillness.
